Frequently asked questions

How do I find out which roads are closed?

Open Road Closures. You can filter by location and status, and every closure shows an alternative route.

How do I report a construction problem?

Go to Submit Complaint, choose a location and category, describe the problem and add a photo if you can. You will get a reference ID straight away.

How do I check on my complaint?

Open Track Complaint and enter your reference ID (for example CMP-0001). You will see its status and any response from our staff.

What do the complaint statuses mean?

Submitted: we have received it. In Progress: staff are working on it or have assigned it to a team. Resolved: the issue has been dealt with and a final response is recorded.

Who can see my personal details?

Only authorised staff. The public tracking page never shows your name or contact details.

Where do announcements come from?

Authorised construction staff publish them. You can search them by keyword, location, category and date on the Announcements page.
